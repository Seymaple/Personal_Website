About Me

Digital Artist specializing in 3D Modeling, Animation, and Visualization

Detailed Bio

My name is Sey Yeaple and I am a digital artist specializing in 3D work. I am a Penn State Graduate in Digital Art Media and Design (DAMD), formally known as IDS (Interdisciplinary Digital Studios).

I currently work for The Grove Center at Eberly College of Science at Penn State. I primarily work on creating 3D assets (modeling/texturing), 3D scans, animations, video compositing, VR/AR, AI education/research, and Data Visualization.

Software Proficiency:
Adobe Creative Suite, Blender, Maya, Substance Painter, Unreal Engine, Marvelous Designer, Medium (VR), and Artec 3D Scanning. I also proficiently use AI tools for education and research purposes.
